Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NEXRA platform.

NEXRA is built on a custom-engineered infrastructure that prioritizes speed and security. Unlike legacy providers, we offer 10k+ msgs/sec throughput, 256-bit AES encryption as standard, and a transparent pricing model with zero hidden platform fees.
Our API is designed for developer happiness. With our comprehensive SDKs and clear documentation, most teams can be up and running in less than 30 minutes. We provide pre-built snippets for Node.js, Python, and Go.
We currently focus on the Ghana market with direct connections to all major networks: MTN, Telecel, and AirtelTigo. This localized infrastructure ensures the highest delivery rates and fastest throughput for Ghanaian businesses.
Security is our top priority. All data at rest is encrypted using 256-bit AES, and data in transit is protected by TLS 1.3. We are GDPR compliant and strictly follow SOC2 security principles to ensure your data remains your data.
